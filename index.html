Luis M. Espinoza Bardales

I am an Assistant Professor at the Department of International Affairs at the Bush School of Government and Public Service at Texas A&M University.

My research lies at the intersection of international trade and economic development. I study the role that foreign affiliates of multinational firms can play in the economic development of their host economies.

I received my Ph.D. in Economics from the University of Michigan in 2024. I also hold a Master in Public Administration in International Development from Harvard Kennedy School.
